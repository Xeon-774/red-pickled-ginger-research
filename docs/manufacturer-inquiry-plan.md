# Manufacturer inquiry plan

Tracked by issue #12. Prepared: 2026-10-02.

## Purpose

Several high-value facts cannot be recovered reliably from current public pages:

- additive concentrations;
- whether a product is legally handled as `酢漬の漬物` for the potassium-sorbate use limit;
- equilibrium/final pH;
- exact solid/drained weight;
- whether two same-name SKUs are predecessor/successor, alternate supplier, or parallel products.

This document provides copy/paste inquiries and a response-recording protocol. **No inquiry has been submitted by this repository work.** Official forms require user identity/contact details.

Canonical tracker:

- `data/manufacturer-inquiries.csv`

## Evidence handling rules

1. Tie every answer to the exact JAN/SKU.
2. Keep units and basis exactly as stated.
   - potassium sorbate may be stated as compound mass or as sorbic-acid equivalent;
   - stevia may be stated as additive mass or steviol equivalent.
3. A response such as "within legal limits" is qualitative only. Do not invent a numeric concentration.
4. "Trade secret / cannot disclose" is a valid result and should be recorded.
5. If a manufacturer gives only an upper specification, use that as an upper bound and label it as such.
6. If pH cannot be disclosed numerically, a statement such as "pH 4.0以下" can still resolve the vinegar-pickle classification question when tied to the product.
7. Do not apply a response for one JAN to another same-name product unless the manufacturer explicitly says the formulations/specifications are identical.

## Why the pickle-category question matters

Japan's potassium-sorbate standard permits up to **0.50 g/kg as sorbic acid** in `酢漬の漬物`.

The Ministry of Health pickle-hygiene definition describes `酢漬` as vegetables etc. pickled mainly with vinegar, ume vinegar, or organic acid and having **pH <=4.0**.

Sources:

- additive standard:
  https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/standards_evaluation/food_additives/second_additive_01/assets/cms_standards103_20240507_11.pdf
- pickle definition:
  https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=00tc3961&dataType=1

A label that merely lists vinegar or acidulant is not enough by itself to prove this classification.

## Response recording template

When a reply arrives, add/update the tracker with:

- response date
- exact JAN/SKU named by the responder
- disclosed value
- unit
- whether it is actual-use level, specification upper limit, or legal limit
- whether the value is expressed as the additive itself or an equivalent basis
- pH statement
- product-category statement
- refusal / trade-secret fields
- concise response summary

Then update only the affected canonical files.
