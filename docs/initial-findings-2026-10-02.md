# Initial findings snapshot — 2026-10-02

This snapshot preserves the first verified facts from the initial research pass. It is intentionally conservative: anything not directly verified is left unknown.

## Methodological conclusions from the first pass

1. Sodium is likely to be a major decision variable for high-intake use.
2. Product form matters: a low-additive flat-slice SKU may differ materially from the same brand's shredded SKU.
3. Synthetic versus natural color is not used as a direct safety ranking.
4. Unknown additive concentration is recorded as uncertainty, not converted into an assumed safe or unsafe exposure.
5. Prices must be captured with date, store/channel, shipping, and solid/net-weight basis before ranking.
6. Hadano shelf prices require a dedicated local survey because chain online prices do not reliably establish local store prices.
