# red-pickled-ginger-research

Evidence-based research on cost, safety, sodium, ingredients, sourcing, storage, and DIY preparation of beni shoga (Japanese red pickled ginger).

## Scope

This repository compares commercial and DIY red pickled ginger options with emphasis on:

- total delivered cost and solid-weight-normalized cost
- sodium content
- additive identity and exposure
- ingredient transparency
- origin and sourcing
- texture and practical eating quality
- storage and bulk-purchase practicality
- availability in Hadano City, Kanagawa
- DIY and rinsing-based sodium reduction

## Decision model

Default comparison assumptions:

- reference adult body mass: 70 kg
- high-intake scenario: 50 g/day
- stress-test scenario: 100 g/day
- additive screening target: <=10% of ADI from one food where a numeric ADI is available
- sodium target for strong preference: <=3 g salt equivalent / 100 g
- price normalization: JPY per 100 g of solid edible portion
- shipping included where applicable
- natural vs synthetic origin is not treated as a safety verdict by itself
- unknown additive concentration is marked uncertain, not guessed
- pH regulators / acidulants may be accepted when safety is established
- country of origin is not scored as inherently safer or less safe without evidence

## Research workflow

1. Record product facts and sources.
2. Separate verified facts from assumptions and estimates.
3. Quantify price, sodium, and additive exposure where data allow.
4. Mark unresolved data as unknown rather than infer values.
5. Compare commercial, bulk, retail, rinsed, and DIY scenarios separately.
6. Keep issue-driven work so findings remain auditable.
